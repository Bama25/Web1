Fairness

Measures the extent to which employees feel that management practices are fair by assessing the equity, impartiality, and justice employees perceive in the workplace.

  • Equity and Impartiality

    • Conduct a team audit to identify repetitive tasks for automation, creating a roadmap to streamline processes.
      • Support Document: Skillsoft course on Process Automation – Digital Automation (pes_11d61870-dc1f-11e7-9fe8-1b7f5fcc733d)
    • Audit existing practices to identify redundancies, encouraging teams to suggest discontinuing ineffective ones to boost efficiency and morale.
      • Support Document: Job aid on “Process Improvement Techniques” – Enabling Process Improvement (pes_9be8d800-b2ab-11e8-8440-17c75fe80c8d)
    • Ensure transparent and consistent application of company policies.
      • Support Document: Skillsoft course on Policy Implementation – Policy on Policies

  • Development Opportunities

    • Pair high-performing teams with those looking to improve for knowledge transfer and collaboration.
      • Support Document: Skillsoft course on Knowledge Transfer – Knowledge Transfer: The Key to Organizational Resilience and Agility: Talent Management, Volume 35, Issue 1807, July 2018 (pes_a607fff8-286d-41cf-8b48-93b2a06ec411)
    • Offer cross-training opportunities to employees to learn different roles.
      • Support Document: Job aid on “Cross-Training Programs”
